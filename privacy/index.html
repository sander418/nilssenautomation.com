Who this covers

People at accounting, bookkeeping and tax firms whom I contact about my service, and people who reply to me. It doesn't cover clients of mine, who get a separate agreement. People who visit this website are covered in their own section below.

  • Name, work email address, firm name, role, and the firm's website or public profile where I found you.

I don't collect anything I don't need to write to you about this service.

Where it comes from

I collect contact details from public sources, such as a firm's own website or a professional directory. I don't buy lists or guess email addresses. Each first email and follow-up says where I found your address. Some earlier research used AI tools, as described under "Earlier use of AI tools".

Why I use it, and my legal basis

I use your details to contact your firm about a document-request setup service and to handle your reply. My legal basis is legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)): contacting firms about a service relevant to their work. I only contact businesses, I send a small number of AI-assisted emails, and I stop as soon as you object. After 25 November 2026, my rule is that AI help with emails is limited to general text and templates that don't identify you. If you think my interest doesn't outweigh yours, tell me.

I don't make decisions about you by automated means that have legal or similarly significant effects.

Who sees it

  • Google Workspace (email and storage), which acts as my processor.
  • My rule after 25 November 2026 is that I read and answer replies and send emails myself, without giving my mailbox, replies or emails to AI tools. I may use Claude to update my contact list, as described below.

Its policy describes relying on adequacy decisions and Standard Contractual Clauses for such transfers. My setting for training its models is off; its policies can still allow some uses.

I don't sell your details, and I use them only for the purposes described here.

For transfers to the United States, Google says it uses the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, under which Google LLC is certified. For other transfers that need a safeguard, its data processing terms provide for another recognised transfer solution or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long I keep it

Your rights

You can ask me to show you what I hold about you, correct it, delete it, or restrict how I use it. To do any of this, reply "no thanks" to my email or write to sander@nilssenautomation.com. I'll answer within 30 days.

If you're in the EU/EEA, you can also complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet), datatilsynet.no, or to your local authority.
