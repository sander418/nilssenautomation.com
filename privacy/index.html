Who this covers

People at accounting, bookkeeping and tax firms whom I contact about my service, and people who reply to me. It doesn't cover clients of mine, who get a separate agreement.

What I collect

  • Name, work email address, firm name, role, and the firm's website or public profile where I found you.
  • Anything you write in a reply to me.
  • A note of when I contacted you and whether you asked me to stop.

I don't collect anything I don't need to write to you about this service.

Where it comes from

I collect contact details by hand from public sources, such as a firm's own website or a professional directory. I don't buy lists, use scraping tools or guess email addresses. Each email I send says where I found your address.

Why I use it, and my legal basis

I use your details to contact your firm about a document-request setup service and to handle your reply. My legal basis is legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)): contacting firms about a service relevant to their work. I only contact businesses, I send a small number of hand-written emails, and I stop as soon as you object. If you think my interest doesn't outweigh yours, tell me.

Who sees it

  • Google Workspace (email and storage), which acts as my processor.
  • Nobody else. I don't sell or share your details, and I don't use them for anything else.

Because Google runs its services internationally, your data may be handled outside the EU/EEA, under the safeguards Google provides for business customers.

How long I keep it

  • Up to 12 months after my last message to you or your last reply, then I delete it.
  • If you ask me to stop, I keep only a minimal "do not contact" record (your email address and the date) so I don't contact you again.

Your rights

You can ask me to show you what I hold about you, correct it, or delete it. You can object to me using your details for outreach, and I'll stop and add you to the "do not contact" record. To do any of this, reply "no thanks" to my email or write to sander@nilssenautomation.com.

If you're in the EU/EEA, you can also complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet), datatilsynet.no, or to your local authority.

Changes

I'll update this page if what I do with your data changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
